Our Story

The Streets of Lagos, Right Here in Windsor

StreetGrillz is a Nigerian street food restaurant serving authentic Halal grilled favourites, bold West African flavours, and freshly prepared comfort meals right here in Windsor, Ontario.

Inspired by the vibrant street food culture of Nigeria, we bring smoky grills, rich spices, and casual dining together in one place — from suya-spiced grilled meats and flame-grilled dishes, to smoky jollof rice, Nigerian pies, and tropical drinks.

Every dish is crafted to carry you to the bustling evening food stalls of Lagos and Abuja — where the smoke rises, the skewers sizzle, and every bite tells a story.
